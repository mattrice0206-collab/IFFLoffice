Article I – Team Names

All team names must be Jewish or Israeli related. Be creative. Be funny. If the Commissioner decides your name is unacceptable, you will be required to change it.

Article II – League Communication

All league announcements, rule changes, scheduling, voting, trade discussions, complaints, league business, and general nonsense must take place here.

If it isn't posted in this server, it didn't happen.

Article III – Buy-In & Prize Pool

The annual buy-in shall be $5.00 USD per team.

The buy-in is subject to change in future seasons following league approval.

Prize Pool

  • 1st Place — $25
  • 2nd Place — $10
  • 3rd Place — $5

Managers who finish in a prize-paying position shall automatically have their winnings applied toward the following season's buy-in unless they specifically request the full payout.

Article IV – Draft Order

Section 1 – Inaugural Season

The draft order for the inaugural season shall be determined by a Draft Lottery.

Section 2 – Future Seasons

Beginning with Season Two:

  • The bottom four teams from the previous season shall each receive equal odds in a lottery to determine the top four draft selections.
  • Draft selections five through eight shall be determined by playoff finish.

Because while tanking is funny, we're going to at least pretend we're trying to discourage it.

Article V – The Draft

The league draft shall be conducted offline using FanDraft in order to create an experience that more closely resembles the NFL Draft...

...except with significantly more bullshit.

Complete draft procedures and timing rules shall be published separately.

Managers unable to attend the draft in person may follow along through the official YouTube livestream.

Article VI – Keepers

Beginning after the inaugural season, each franchise may designate one (1) keeper.

Additional keeper rules may be adopted in future seasons as needed.

Article VII – League Format

League Structure

  • 8 Teams
  • 2 Divisions consisting of 4 teams each
  • 10 Regular Season Matchups
  • Division opponents are played twice
  • Out-of-division opponents are played once

Playoffs

  • Seven (7) teams qualify for the playoffs.
  • The only team that misses the playoffs is the last-place team.
  • If you miss a seven-team playoff, you deserve whatever punishment the league decides.
  • Home teams receive a 3-point Home Field Advantage throughout the playoffs.
  • Playoff seeding tiebreaker: Head-to-Head Record.
  • The playoff bracket shall not reseed after each round.

Roster Composition

  • 1 Quarterback (QB)
  • 2 Running Backs (RB)
  • 2 Wide Receivers (WR)
  • 1 Tight End (TE)
  • 1 FLEX (RB / WR / TE)
  • 1 Team Defense / Special Teams (D/ST)
  • 1 Kicker (K)
  • 7 Bench Players
  • 2 Injured Reserve (IR) Spots

League Settings

  • Full PPR Scoring
  • Individual players lock at their scheduled kickoff time.
  • Free Agent Acquisition Budget (FAAB): $150
  • Minimum FAAB Bid: $1
  • Waiver Period: 1 Day
  • Unlimited player acquisitions.
  • Unlimited trades until the trade deadline.
  • Trade Deadline: November 11, 2026 @ 12:00 PM CST
  • No Undroppable Player List.
  • NFL Player Pool.

Scoring

Article VIII – Last Place Punishment

Each season shall conclude with one unfortunate manager finishing in last place.

The punishment for finishing last shall be determined during the Annual League Meeting prior to the start of each season.

Failure to complete the punishment may result in public ridicule, loss of league privileges, or being called soft for the foreseeable future.

Article IX – Trade Submission Procedure

All trades must be submitted through the official Trade Request Form.

To initiate a trade, both participating managers must independently complete and submit the Trade Request Form. A trade shall not be recognized by the Office of the Commissioner until both submissions have been received and verified.

Upon verification, the Office of the Commissioner will assign the transaction an official Trade Identification Number (Trade ID) and publish the proposed trade in the trade-polls channel.

The trade shall then enter a one (1) day Public Review Period, during which league members may vote on whether the transaction should undergo an official review by the Office of the Commissioner.

If the trade receives four (4) or more "Yes" votes during the Public Review Period, it shall be referred to the Office of the Commissioner for a formal investigation pursuant to the League Trade Review Procedure.

If the trade does not reach the review threshold by the conclusion of the Public Review Period, it shall be deemed approved by the Office of the Commissioner.

Only after receiving official approval may the participating managers execute the trade on ESPN.

Any trade completed on ESPN before receiving approval from the Office of the Commissioner shall be immediately vetoed. Both managers involved shall enter a State of Review, during which the Office of the Commissioner may investigate the circumstances surrounding the unauthorized transaction and determine whether additional disciplinary action is warranted.

Managers are reminded that submitting a Trade Request Form constitutes an official declaration of intent to complete the transaction. Once both parties have submitted matching Trade Request Forms, the trade may not be withdrawn without approval from the Office of the Commissioner.

Article X – Trade Review Procedure

Every completed trade shall be posted by the Office of the Commissioner for public review.

Each trade shall be accompanied by an official poll asking:

Should this trade be reviewed by the Office of the Commissioner?

  • No
  • Yes

Purpose of the Poll

This poll does not approve or reject trades.

Its sole purpose is to determine whether a trade warrants an official review by the Office of the Commissioner.

League members are encouraged to vote honestly based upon whether they believe the transaction may involve:

  • Collusion
  • Bad faith
  • Circumvention of league rules
  • Conduct detrimental to the integrity of the league

Being bad at fantasy football is not grounds for review.

Triggering a Review

If a trade receives four (4) or more "Yes" votes, the transaction shall be referred to the Office of the Commissioner for formal review.

The Commissioner shall issue one of the following rulings:

  • Approved — The trade is valid and shall process as scheduled.
  • Approved with Warning — The trade shall process, but the managers involved shall receive guidance regarding future transactions.
  • Rejected — The trade violates league rules or is determined to involve collusion or other conduct detrimental to the integrity of the league.

The Commissioner's ruling shall be final pending appeal.

Commissioner Review Standards

The Office of the Commissioner shall not overturn a trade simply because it is unpopular or uneven.

Fantasy managers have the right to make bad trades.

Trades shall only be overturned if credible evidence exists that they involve:

  • Collusion
  • Intentional league sabotage
  • Fraudulent conduct
  • Other violations of the League Constitution

The burden of proof rests with those requesting the review.

Commissioner's Authority

Nothing in this Article limits the authority of the Office of the Commissioner to initiate an official review of any trade at any time, regardless of the results of the public review poll or whether the trade has already been approved.

If credible evidence of collusion, fraud, bad faith, or any other violation of the League Constitution becomes available, the Office of the Commissioner may reopen, investigate, and, if necessary, overturn any transaction in order to preserve the integrity of the league.

Managers are reminded that disagreeing with a trade is not the same as proving it should be overturned.

The Commissioner protects the integrity of the league—not your fantasy team.

Article XI – Discipline

The Office of the Commissioner is responsible for preserving the integrity, fairness, and overall enjoyment of the league. While this league is intended to be fun, managers are expected to conduct themselves in a manner consistent with good sportsmanship and the League Constitution.

Disciplinary action may be taken against any manager who engages in conduct detrimental to the league, including, but not limited to:

  • Collusion.
  • Intentional league sabotage.
  • Repeated violations of the League Constitution or Draft Procedures.
  • Abuse of league systems or processes.
  • Failure to comply with official rulings of the Office of the Commissioner.
  • Completing unauthorized transactions outside of established league procedures.
  • Any other conduct deemed detrimental to the integrity of the league.

The Office of the Commissioner may impose one or more of the following disciplinary actions depending upon the severity of the violation:

  • Official Verbal Warning
  • Official Written Warning
  • Reversal of an unauthorized transaction
  • Loss of draft capital
  • Loss of waiver priority or FAAB (if applicable)
  • Removal of keeper privileges
  • Monetary fine payable to the league prize pool
  • Temporary suspension of trading privileges
  • Temporary suspension from league activities
  • Expulsion from the league

Nothing in this Article requires the Office of the Commissioner to issue discipline in any particular order. Depending on the nature of the offense, disciplinary action may be immediate.

Managers subject to disciplinary action retain the right to file an Official Appeal pursuant to the League Appeal Procedure.

The Office of the Commissioner reserves the authority to investigate any alleged violation of the League Constitution, whether reported through an official form or discovered independently.

All disciplinary decisions shall be documented by the Office of the Commissioner and, when appropriate, published in the official Commissioner Rulings for the purpose of maintaining transparency and establishing league precedent.

Managers are reminded that while mistakes happen, intentional misconduct will be addressed. The purpose of discipline is not to punish managers for making bad fantasy football decisions—it is to protect the integrity of the league.

Article XII – Annual League Meeting

An Annual League Meeting shall be held every August over Discord.

The date and time shall be announced by the Office of the Commissioner.

Meeting Agenda

  • Scheduling the annual draft.
  • Determining the last-place punishment.
  • Discussing and voting on amendments to the League Constitution.
  • Addressing any additional league business.
  • Arguing over things that ultimately don't matter.

Attendance is strongly encouraged.

Complaining about decisions made while absent remains your constitutional right, but nobody is obligated to care.

Article XIII – Amendments

The Commissioner reserves the right to interpret this Constitution in the dumbest reasonable way possible.

Any amendment to this Constitution shall be proposed, discussed, and voted upon during the Annual League Meeting.

Upon league approval, the amendment shall become an official part of this Constitution and shall remain in effect until subsequently amended or repealed.

Article XIV – Final Declaration

By participating in this league, you acknowledge that fantasy football is approximately 30% skill, 70% luck, and 100% losing because your opponent's backup tight end had the game of his life.

You further acknowledge that screenshots are forever, the group chat never forgets, and every terrible trade will be brought up for years to come.

May the least incompetent manager win.


Signed,

The Office of the Commissioner
