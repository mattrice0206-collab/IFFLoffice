League Overview

  • 8 Teams
  • 2 Divisions (4 Teams Each)
  • 10 Regular Season Games
  • Division opponents play twice.
  • Out-of-division opponents play once.

Roster Requirements

  • 1 Quarterback (QB)
  • 2 Running Backs (RB)
  • 2 Wide Receivers (WR)
  • 1 Tight End (TE)
  • 1 FLEX (RB / WR / TE)
  • 1 Team Defense / Special Teams (D/ST)
  • 1 Kicker (K)
  • 7 Bench Players
  • 2 Injured Reserve (IR) Spots

Playoffs

  • Seven (7) teams qualify.
  • Last place misses the playoffs.
  • Home teams receive a 3-point Home Field Advantage.
  • Head-to-Head is the primary tiebreaker.
  • The bracket does not reseed.

Draft

  • Season One draft order is determined by lottery.
  • Beginning in Season Two, the bottom four teams enter a draft lottery for Picks 1–4.
  • Picks 5–8 are determined by playoff finish.
  • The annual draft is conducted using FanDraft.

Keepers

  • Each franchise may designate one keeper beginning after Season One.
  • Additional keeper rules may be adopted by the league.

Trades

  • All trades must be submitted through the official Trade Request Form.
  • Both managers must submit matching forms.
  • Trades undergo a one-day public review period.
  • Four or more review votes trigger a Commissioner's Review.
  • Only approved trades may be completed on ESPN.
  • Unauthorized trades are subject to veto and investigation.

Waivers & Free Agency

  • FAAB Budget: $150
  • Minimum Bid: $1
  • One-Day Waiver Period
  • Unlimited Player Acquisitions

League Settings

  • Full PPR Scoring
  • Players lock at kickoff.
  • Unlimited trades until the trade deadline.
  • Trade Deadline: November 11, 2026 — 12:00 PM CST
  • No Undroppable Player List.
  • NFL Player Pool.

Scoring Summary

Offense

  • Passing TD: 4
  • Rushing TD: 6
  • Receiving TD: 6
  • Reception: 1
  • Passing Yards: 1 point per 25 yards
  • Rushing / Receiving Yards: 1 point per 10 yards
  • Interceptions Thrown: -2
  • Two-Point Conversion: 2

Kicking

  • PAT: 1
  • FG 0–39: 3
  • FG 40–49: 4
  • FG 50–59: 5
  • FG 60+: 6
  • Missed FG: -1

Defense / Special Teams

  • Defensive TD: 6
  • Sack: 1
  • Interception: 2
  • Fumble Recovery: 2
  • Safety: 2
  • Blocked Kick: 2

For complete defensive scoring (Points Allowed and Yards Allowed), refer to the League Constitution or ESPN League Settings.

Last Place

The last-place manager shall complete the league punishment selected during the Annual League Meeting.

Commissioner Authority

The Office of the Commissioner is responsible for enforcing league rules, interpreting the League Constitution, protecting the integrity of the league, and issuing official rulings.

This page serves as a quick-reference guide. In the event of any conflict between these Rules and the League Constitution, the Constitution shall control.
