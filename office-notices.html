OFFICE NOTICE 2026-001

League Website Established

Issued: August 2026

The Office of the Commissioner is pleased to announce the launch of the official Israeli Fantasy Football League website.

This website serves as the official repository for league documents, Commissioner rulings, memorandums, notices, forms, and historical records.

Managers should consider information published on this website to be official unless superseded by a subsequent notice.
