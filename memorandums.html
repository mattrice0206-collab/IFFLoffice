MEMORANDUM 2026-001
Purpose of the Office of the Commissioner
Issued: August 2026

The Office of the Commissioner exists to preserve the integrity, fairness, transparency, and long-term stability of the Israeli Fantasy Football League.

The Commissioner is not responsible for managing individual fantasy teams, evaluating trades based on personal opinion, or preventing poor roster decisions. Fantasy managers retain the right to build, manage, and, if they choose, completely destroy their own teams.

The Commissioner's responsibility is to administer league procedures fairly, enforce the League Constitution, investigate potential misconduct, resolve disputes, and maintain confidence in league operations.

Whenever possible, the Office of the Commissioner shall act consistently with established precedent and publish significant decisions to promote transparency throughout the league.

Issued By: Office of the Commissioner
