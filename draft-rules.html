Official Draft Procedures

These procedures govern the annual Israeli Fantasy Football League Player Draft and are administered by the Office of the Commissioner.

Article I – Draft Date

The official draft date and time shall be determined during the Annual League Meeting.

Managers are expected to make every reasonable effort to attend.

Managers unable to attend in person may participate remotely through approved league channels.

Article II – Draft Format

The annual player draft shall consist of:

  • Sixteen (16) Rounds
  • Snake Draft Format
  • One (1) Minute and Thirty (30) Seconds per Selection

The draft will be conducted offline using FanDraft to provide a professional NFL Draft experience.

Article III – Draft Attendance

Managers attending remotely may participate through the official Draft Day voice channel on Discord.

The draft will also be live streamed on YouTube for managers unable to attend in person.

Article IV – Draft Selection Procedure

All draft selections shall remain unofficial until approved by the Office of the Commissioner.

When making a selection, managers shall privately message the Commissioner on Discord with the following information:

  • Draft Pick Number
  • Player Name
  • NFL Team
  • Position

Upon verification, the Commissioner shall officially announce the selection.

The Office of the Commissioner reserves the authority to reject any submission that is incomplete, unclear, inaccurate, or otherwise incomprehensible.

Article V – Early Draft Selections

Managers may not submit draft selections before they are officially on the clock.

Any player submitted before a manager's selection window opens shall be disregarded.

Should a manager continue attempting to submit selections prematurely, the Commissioner may make the selection on that manager's behalf.

Patience is a virtue.

Article VI – Draft Timeouts

Each franchise shall receive three (3) timeouts during the draft.

Timeout Regulations

  • Each timeout lasts thirty (30) seconds.
  • Timeouts may only be used while the manager is officially on the clock.
  • Timeout requests must be submitted privately to the Commissioner on Discord.

The Office of the Commissioner recognizes that many managers participate in multiple fantasy football leagues and that draft preparation occasionally becomes... inadequate.

Managers requiring assistance selecting a player may request guidance from the Commissioner during an official timeout.

Illegal Timeout Usage

Managers attempting to use a timeout after exhausting all three allotted timeouts shall immediately forfeit control of their current selection.

The Commissioner shall make the selection on the manager's behalf.

Actions have consequences.

Article VII – Draft Clock Violations

Managers are expected to make their selection before the expiration of the draft clock.

First Offense

If a manager fails to submit a selection before time expires, the Office of the Commissioner shall immediately make the draft selection on that manager's behalf using its best judgment.

Second Offense

If the same manager allows the draft clock to expire a second time during the draft, that manager shall forfeit their current selection.

The forfeited selection shall be recorded as a skipped pick, and the draft shall immediately proceed to the next franchise.

Managers may continue participating with their next scheduled draft selection.

Article VIII – Draft Documentation

During the Annual League Meeting, each manager shall provide the following information for official league records:

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Discord Username

This information is maintained solely for official league documentation.

No, the Office of the Commissioner does not have anything particularly important planned for it. It simply makes the records look more official.

Article IX – Commissioner Authority

The Office of the Commissioner shall oversee all draft operations.

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  • Approving all selections
  • Announcing all selections
  • Managing the draft clock
  • Approving timeout requests
  • Resolving disputes
  • Correcting administrative errors
  • Pretending this is significantly more important than it actually is

The Commissioner's ruling during the draft shall be final.

Appeals may be submitted after the completion of the draft using the Official Appeal Form.

Article X – Amendments

These Draft Procedures may be amended at any time before the commencement of the annual player draft.

Any substantive amendments shall be announced through the official league Discord server and published on the league website.

Final Notice

The Office of the Commissioner reminds all managers that these procedures exist to simulate the professionalism and excitement of the NFL Draft.

Unfortunately, the participants remain fantasy football managers.

Good luck.

Draft wisely.

There are no bad picks... until someone else screenshots them.
