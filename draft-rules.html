Annual Draft Procedures

Official Draft Operations Manual

Authority

These Draft Procedures supplement the League Constitution and govern the administration of the annual IFFL Draft.

Draft Format

  • Offline Live Draft using FanDraft.
  • Snake Draft Format.
  • Each franchise selects one player per round.
  • The Commissioner serves as Draft Administrator.

Draft Order

  • Season One draft order is determined by Draft Lottery.
  • Future seasons follow the procedures established in the League Constitution.

Manager Attendance

  • Managers are expected to attend the draft.
  • Managers unable to attend may participate remotely through the official livestream or other approved method.
  • If a manager cannot make a selection, they are responsible for providing a draft board or representative.

Draft Clock

  • The Commissioner shall announce the draft clock before the draft begins.
  • Managers are expected to make selections promptly.
  • Reasonable flexibility may be granted to maintain the pace of the event.

Player Eligibility

  • Any NFL player available within the ESPN player pool is eligible for selection.
  • Once drafted, a player belongs exclusively to that franchise.

Trade Activity

  • Draft pick trades must be approved before the draft begins.
  • Any trade occurring during the draft remains subject to League Trade Procedures.

Technical Issues

  • The Commissioner may temporarily pause the draft in the event of technical difficulties.
  • If necessary, the draft may resume after the issue has been resolved.

Commissioner's Authority

The Office of the Commissioner retains the authority to resolve procedural disputes, correct administrative errors, and ensure the orderly operation of the annual draft.

Sportsmanship

Trash talk is encouraged.

Delaying the draft, intentionally disrupting proceedings, or acting in bad faith is not.

Remember: everyone thinks they had the best draft. Most of you are wrong.
