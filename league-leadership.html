League Leadership

The Israeli Fantasy Football League is administered by the Office of the Commissioner. The Office is responsible for enforcing the League Constitution, maintaining competitive integrity, administering league operations, and ensuring a fair, transparent, and enjoyable experience for every manager.

Matthew Rice

Founding Commissioner

Biography

Matthew Rice serves as the founding Commissioner of the Israeli Fantasy Football League, bringing over a decade of fantasy football experience to the position. Throughout the years, he has participated in numerous league formats, including redraft, dynasty, keeper, and salary-cap leagues, while also serving as commissioner for multiple fantasy football leagues.

Outside of fantasy football, Matthew enjoys playing video games and following a variety of professional and collegiate sports. He is a lifelong supporter of the Kansas City Chiefs, Kansas City Royals, St. Louis Blues, Kansas Jayhawks, and the Minnesota Timberwolves. Although football is a major passion, baseball remains his favorite sport.

As Commissioner, Matthew founded the Israeli Fantasy Football League with the goal of creating an organized, competitive, and transparent league where every manager is treated fairly. Through the League Constitution and the Office of the Commissioner, he is committed to ensuring that league decisions are consistent, impartial, and made in the best interests of the league as a whole.

Responsibilities

  • Constitution Enforcement
  • Official League Rulings
  • Annual Draft Administration
  • Trade Review
  • Appeals
  • Disciplinary Proceedings
  • Website Administration
  • League Records Management

Commissioner's Statement

"My goal as Commissioner is to build a league that is organized, competitive, transparent, and enjoyable for every manager. Every decision made by the Office of the Commissioner will be guided by fairness, consistency, and the long-term success of the Israeli Fantasy Football League. I look forward to serving this league with professionalism and impartiality for many seasons to come."

Javen Arndt

Founding Co-Commissioner

Biography

With more than a decade of fantasy football experience, Javen Arndt brings a deep passion for the game and a proven track record of success to the Israeli’s Fantasy Football League. A three-time fantasy football champion across his primary leagues, Javen has built a reputation for strategic roster management, year-round dedication, and a relentless competitive spirit. His experience as both a player and league member provides valuable insight into what it takes to create an engaging and rewarding fantasy football experience.

Outside of fantasy football, Javen is pursuing a career in sports broadcasting while studying Journalism and Mass Communications with a concentration in Sports Media and Society, along with a minor in Sport Management, at the University of Kansas. A lifelong sports fan, he proudly supports all KU athletics, the Kansas City Chiefs, Kansas City Royals, and the Minnesota Timberwolves. His passion for sports extends beyond fandom, driving his ambition to tell the stories that make sports meaningful.

As the Founding Co-Commissioner, Javen works alongside Founding Commissioner Matthew Rice to help maintain a competitive, well-organized, and enjoyable experience within the Israeli’s Fantasy Football League. Together, they are committed to building a league culture centered on fairness, activity, and excellence, with the goal of setting the standard for what a fantasy football league should be.

Responsibilities

  • Manager Relations and Support
  • Reviews League Procedures and Policies
  • Acts on behalf of the Commissioner when necessary
  • Assists with Website Administration
  • Promotes League Growth and Engagement
  • Helps Preserve Competitive Integrity

Co-Commissioner's Statement

"It is an honor to serve as the Founding Co-Commissioner of the Israeli’s Fantasy Football League. My goal is to help bridge the league’s two home bases in El Dorado and Lawrence, Kansas, creating one connected and competitive community for every manager. Alongside Founding Commissioner Rice, I am committed to maintaining competitive integrity, encouraging year-round engagement, and continually improving the league experience. Together, we are building a league that strives to set the standard for fantasy football around the world. I look forward to helping make the Israeli’s Fantasy Football League the premier destination for passionate fantasy football players."

Office of the Commissioner

The Office of the Commissioner serves as the governing authority of the Israeli Fantasy Football League. Operating under the League Constitution, the Office is responsible for administering league operations, enforcing league rules, resolving disputes, and preserving the integrity of competition.

Every official action taken by the Office is guided by the principles of fairness, consistency, transparency, and professionalism.
